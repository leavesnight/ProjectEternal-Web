Project Eternal Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026 · Applicable version: v0.1.0

Version v0.1.0 is a single-player build. It does not require account registration, contain ads or in-app purchases, or provide developer-operated online accounts or multiplayer services.

1. Information We Process

The current version does not actively collect, upload, or store personal information that directly identifies users. Local settings, runtime state, or save data may be stored on the user's device.

2. Device Permissions and XR Functions

Project Eternal uses headset and controller pose, orientation, and input data for camera movement, locomotion, interaction, and mecha controls. The current version does not use microphone, camera capture, contacts, or precise location permissions to collect personal information.

3. Third-Party Platforms

Project Eternal is developed with Unity and OpenXR and may run on compatible platforms such as PICO. Platform-level services may be processed under the platform provider's own privacy policy.

4. Storage and Transmission

Game data actively handled by us is primarily stored locally. We do not currently operate account, advertising, analytics, or cloud-save servers for v0.1.0.

5. Minors

Project Eternal is not designed to collect personal information from minors. Minors should use the application under guardian guidance.

6. Future Features

Future versions may add room-code multiplayer, cloud saves, accounts, crash reporting, or other online capabilities. This Policy will be updated before such features are released if they introduce new data processing.

7. Security and Updates

We apply reasonable safeguards appropriate to the scale of the project and may update this Policy as the product, platform rules, or laws change.
